02 · The problem

Chronic illness has three
invisible crises.

I. Memory
"How long has this been going on?"
And you can't remember. The honest answer is months, but the dates, the patterns, the start — gone in the appointment haze.
II. Communication
"It hurts a lot."
Doesn't convey severity, pattern, or impact. Fifteen minutes rarely leave room to translate experience into clinical signal.
III. Coordination
"Did you tell rheumatology?"
Multiple providers, scattered records, no one sees the whole picture. You become the unpaid integration layer for your own body.
