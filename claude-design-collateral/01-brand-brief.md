# Translate Health — Brand & Design Brief

## Positioning

Translate Health is an **AI health coordination platform** for people navigating chronic illness, complex conditions, or simply trying to understand their own bodies better.

**The core promise:** You talk naturally. AI agents structure, correlate, and translate your health data into something providers can act on — and something *you* can understand.

**Tagline:** "You talk. We translate. Doctors listen."

**Secondary tagline (expanded):** "Your personal health intelligence team."

## The Problem We Solve

Chronic illness patients face three invisible crises:
1. **The memory crisis** — "How long has this been going on?" — and you can't remember
2. **The communication crisis** — "It hurts a lot" doesn't convey severity, pattern, or impact
3. **The coordination crisis** — Multiple providers, scattered records, no one sees the whole picture

## The Solution

An agent-mediated health platform where:
- **Natural conversation** becomes structured health data
- **AI agents** detect patterns humans miss (sleep → pain correlations, medication timing effects)
- **Provider reports** generate on demand, formatted for clinical review
- **The system adapts** to your life — travel, appointments, flare-ups, recovery

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Chronic illness patients (autoimmune, chronic pain, dysautonomia, multi-system conditions)
**Secondary:** Health-conscious individuals tracking longevity, fitness, or preventive care
**Tertiary (B2B):** Providers and practices who want better patient data

## Tone & Voice

- **Human, not clinical.** Warm but precise. Like a smart friend who happens to be medically literate.
- **Empowering, not pitying.** This is a tool for people who are already fighting. We make them stronger.
- **Transparent, not magic.** We explain what the AI is doing and why. No black boxes.
- **Action-oriented.** Every insight should lead to a conversation, a question, or a decision.

## Competitor Landscape

- **Traditional health apps** (MyFitnessPal, Apple Health) — Data entry is work. No intelligence.
- **Symptom trackers** (Bearable, Symple) — Better, but still manual. No provider integration.
- **AI health coaches** (Noom, K Health) — Conversational but narrow. No deep correlation or reporting.
- **EHR portals** (MyChart) — Read-only. Patient-unfriendly. No pattern detection.

**Our differentiator:** We don't just track. We *translate* — from raw experience to structured insight to clinical communication.

## Visual Direction

**Feel:** Clean, calm, trustworthy. Medical but not sterile. Think Aesop meets Notion.

**Color palette:**
- Primary: Deep sage green (#2c5f2d) — growth, health, calm
- Secondary: Warm cream (#faf8f5) — approachable, human
- Accent: Soft coral (#e07a5f) — urgency when needed, warmth
- Neutral: Charcoal (#2d2d2d) — text, structure
- Light: Soft gray-blue (#e8e9eb) — backgrounds, cards

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Clean sans-serif, medium weight (Inter or similar)
- Body: Readable, slightly warm (system sans or Inter)
- Data/numbers: Monospace for metrics, clean sans for labels

**Imagery:**
- Abstract organic shapes (referencing cellular structures, neural networks)
- Soft gradients (referencing data visualization)
- Real people, real moments (not stock-photo-smiling-patients)
- Screenshots of the actual product interface

## Must-Convey on Homepage

1. **What it is** in one sentence
2. **How it works** in 3-4 simple steps
3. **Who it's for** (with specificity — chronic illness isn't generic)
4. **Trust signals** (privacy-first, HIPAA-aligned, data ownership)
5. **Call to action** (join waitlist, see demo, learn more)
