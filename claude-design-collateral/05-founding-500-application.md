**What have you built with agents in the last 6 months?**

I've built and deployed a personal health intelligence system that coordinates 7 specialized AI agents to manage chronic illness tracking, pattern detection, and clinical communication. The system has been running in production for 6+ months, handling 3x daily health check-ins via SMS/WhatsApp, generating specialty-tailored provider reports (neurology, rheumatology, primary care), and surfacing cross-variable correlations (sleep → pain, medication timing → BP) that have changed clinical conversations.

Key shipped components:
- **Smart Check-in Engine:** Context-aware prompt generation that adapts to recent data, boosting compliance by making check-ins feel relevant rather than robotic
- **Natural Language Parser:** Extracts structured health data (BP, pain severity, sleep, fatigue) from conversational text with confidence scoring
- **Correlation Analyzer:** Detects cross-day relationships (e.g., "nights with 3+ interruptions → 2.3x higher next-day pain")
- **Provider Report Generator:** Auto-generates specialty-tailored visit prep documents (HTML/PDF) with statistical summaries, trend sparklines, and clinical flags
- **Gap Escalation System:** Detects missed check-ins and generates gentle re-engagement prompts — critical for ADHD patients who fall off tracking routines
- **Epic CCDA Parser:** Imports lab results, medications, and diagnoses from MyChart exports
- **Dashboard Generator:** Single-file HTML dashboards with Chart.js visualizations

The system runs entirely on my personal stack (OpenClaw, local LLMs, Supabase) and has processed 200+ check-ins, generated 15+ provider reports, and identified clinically relevant patterns that have been discussed in actual appointments.
